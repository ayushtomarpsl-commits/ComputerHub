hinglish Day 4 of building plan b for software engineers. Agar aap ek software engineer hain to aapko 9 to 5 ki salary se baahar sochana hi padega, kyonki AI ki vajah se tech industry bahut tezi se badal rahi hai. Aaj main aapke lie ek aisa app idea lekar aaya hoon jo bhaarat ki sabse badi daily problem ko solve karta hai. Parking ki samasya, aapne blaabla car ka naam to suna hi hoga, jahaan log apni car ki khaali seats share karte hain. Bilkul usi concept par aap bana sakte hain. Blaabla car for parking. Aaj har bade shahar mein railway station, markets aur hospitals ke baahar parking milna naamumkin hai. Log ghanton bhatkate hain aur nau parking mein bhaari chaalaan katva baithate hain. Is app ke zarie koi bhi insaan apne ghar ke saamne ki khaali jagah, plot ya dukaan ke aage ka space list kar sakta hai. Aur jab bhi kisi driver ko us ilaake mein parking chaahie hogi, app use sabse nazdiki khaali jagah aur directions dikha dega. Shuruaat mein aapko koi complex payment gateway bhi nahin chaahie. Driver sidhe jagah ke maalik ko UPI ya cash de sakta hai. Is tarah ka poora MVP aap cloud aur cursor ki madad se kuchh hi dinon mein code kar sakte hain. Agar aapko is parking app ka poora system design aur step by step guide chaahie to comment mein likho parking. Main sidhe aapke DM mein bhej doonga aur is series ke final part yaani day paanch ke lie mujhe abhi follow kar lijie. nan

Aapko yah galti bilkul nahin karni. Apne distribution ko tin phase mein baanto. Pahla phase hai communities. Redit ke r slash side project aur x par jao. Vahaan sidhe bechane ki koshish mat karo, balki logon ko batao ki aapne yah extension kis problem ko solve karne ke lie banai hai. Doosra phase, competitor review mining. Yah sabse powerful tarika hai. Apne competitors ke chrome store reviews padho aur samajho ki users ko kis baat se pareshaani ho rahi hai. Jaise tool bahut mahanga hai, ya koi feature thik se kaam nahin kar raha. Ab usi problem ko apni extension mein solve karo. Aur uska ek chhota demo video social media par share karo. Tisra phase hai directories. Toolify aur tiny launch jaisi directories par apna tool list karo aur shuruaati users ke lie ek special launch discount rakho aur agar aap bhi apni 9 to 5 job ke saath ek solid plan bhi banaana chaahte hain to mujhe abhi follow kar lijie. Kyonki yahaan ham har din lekar aaainge nae software ideas, AI tools aur business opportunities. Chalo milte hain day tin mein. Ek bilkul nae. Idea ke saath.

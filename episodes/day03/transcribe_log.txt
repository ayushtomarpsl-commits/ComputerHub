Aaj main aapko ek aisa real micro SAS idea bata raha hoon, jise aap AI ki madad se 0 cost mein bana sakte ho, aur iske lie market mein paying customers pahle se taiyaar baithe hain. Bhaarat mein laakhon log Amazon, Flipkart aur Misho par selling kar rahe hain, lekin har mahine unki sabse badi sirdaarti hoti hai GSTR 1 file karna. E commerce portal se jo sales report download hoti hai, vah sarkaari GST portal sidhe accept nahin karta. Chhote sellers na to har mahine mahange CA ka kharcha utha sakte hain, na hi unhen ghanton excel mein data saaph karna aata hai. Isi problem ko solve karne ke lie aap ek simple web tool bana sakte ho. Yah tool kisi bhi e commerce report ko sirph ek click mein GST complaint excel ya jason file mein convert kar deta hai. Ise aap cloud aur cursor ki madad se kuchh hi ghanton mein bana sakte ho, aur Versal ya onrender dot com par mupht mein deploy kar sakte ho. Business model bilkul sidha hai. Shuruaati sellers ko tis din ka free trial do, aur uske baad sirph do sau ninyaanve se chaar sau ninyaanve rupe ka monthly subscription. Ki mahine ki tis se pachaas hazaar rupe ki fix passive income shuroo. Agar aapko is tool ko step by step banaane ki poori guide aur architecture chaahie, to comment mein likho GST. Main sidhe aapke DM mein bhej doonga, aur apne software career ka solid plan bhi banaane ke lie abhi follow kar lijie.

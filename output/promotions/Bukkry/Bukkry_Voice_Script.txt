₹25,000 ki website banwayi, aur Google pe page 5 pe bhi nahi milti. Matlab customer aapko kabhi dekhega hi nahi.

Aur end mein ek 10 second ka test, jisse pata chalega ki Google aapki website ko dekh bhi raha hai ya nahi.

Galti designer ki nahi hai. Ye 5 wajah hain.

Wajah number 1: Google ne aapka page index hi nahi kiya. Index nahi, to search mein aayega hi nahi.

Number 2: Aapne page pe likha "Best Services", aur customer search karta hai "Noida mein AC repair". Jo shabd log type karte hain, wo aapke page pe hai hi nahi.

Number 3: Aapko pata hi nahi ki kis keyword pe aap kis number pe ho. To sudhaaroge kya?

Number 4: Jo aapse upar hai, wo kaun-se pages bana raha hai, wo aapne kabhi dekha hi nahi.

Aur number 5, isi pe sabse zyada log fanste hain, kyunki ye dikhta hi nahi. Aapke hi do pages ek hi keyword ke liye aapas mein lad rahe hain. Google confuse hota hai, aur dono kamzor pad jaate hain.

Ab wo 10 second ka test. Google kholo, likho site: aur phir apni website ka naam. Ek bhi result nahi aaya? To Google ne aapki website abhi tak index hi nahi ki.

Aapki website Google pe page 5 pe hai, aur customer seedha aapke competitor ke paas ja raha hai.

Problem aapka business nahi hai. Problem ye hai ki aapko pata hi nahi, Google aapki website ko dekh kaise raha hai.

To aaj ek aisi website dikhata hoon, jahan SEO ka har kaam ek hi dashboard mein hai. ai.bukkry.in

Pehla: Position Finder. Aapka page kis keyword pe, kis number pe hai, seedha dekho.
Doosra: Keyword Research. Log Google pe asal mein kya type kar rahe hain, wo nikalo.
Teesra: Competitor Mapper. Aapka competitor kya kar raha hai, usko map karo.
Chautha: Cannibalization. Aapke hi do pages ek keyword ke liye aapas mein lad rahe hain? Ye tool pakadta hai.
Paanchvaan: Indexability Check. Google aapka page index kar bhi pa raha hai, ya nahi.

Aur asli game hai Programmatic SEO. Ek template se alag-alag city aur keyword ke pages ek saath banao. Har page haath se likhne ki zaroorat nahi.

Blog chahiye? AI Blog Engine. Sitemap chahiye? Sitemap Gen. Aur Content Detector batata hai ki aapka content AI jaisa to nahi lag raha.

Landing page banani hai? AI Landing Page Copilot mein layout chuno: Standard, Marketing ya Split.

Leads ke liye AI LeadFinder aur AI Emailer. Aur roz ke kaam ke liye Image, PDF, URL aur WiFi tools alag se.

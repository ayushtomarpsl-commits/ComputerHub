## 1. Audience insight
Small online sellers and freelance devs **doubt that a free GitHub UPI gateway works for real**. Their words:
"working hai bro?", "install successful?", "guide karenge aap?". Emotion: fear of wasting time.

## 2. Best content angle
**Proof first.** Open on real chats from people who installed it, then explain it in one breath, then the Shopify
twist, then "do it yourself or get it set up".

## 6. Retention improvements (done in the script above)
- Drop risk at 16–25 s (explanation) → kept to one breath, motion graphic changes every 2 s.
- Strongest curiosity line: "ab dekho, log mujhe kya bhej rahe hain" (open loop, paid off at 4 s).
- Pattern interrupt with a reason: Shopify "sabse bada update" at 27 s (new info, new colour).
- Cut: star count from the voice (shown on screen instead); no long intro / no "welcome" line.
- Proof order = strongest first (working perfectly + install yes), weakest chats left out.

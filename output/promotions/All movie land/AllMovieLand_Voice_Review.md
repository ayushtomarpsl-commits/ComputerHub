## 2. What is not OK, and why
| # | Line / claim | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Bollywood ki **nayi release**, Hollywood action, South blockbuster — **bilkul free**" | Promises new, paid films for free. That is what a piracy app offers. Streaming or promoting films without a licence = copyright infringement (Copyright Act 1957, s.51 / s.63) and film piracy under the Cinematograph (Amendment) Act 2023 (jail + heavy fine). Play Store also removes such apps. | **Removed.** No film names, no "new release", no "every movie" promise. Talk about app features only. |
| 2 | "OTT subscription par **paise barbaad**… **mehenge subscription ke paise kyon dena?**" | Tells people to stop paying for licensed services and use this instead. Puts down other brands and pushes piracy. Against the ASCI code (no unfair comparison/denigration). | **Removed.** New hook is about a common problem (wasting time finding a movie), not about other apps. |
| 3 | "**100% muft**", "**unlimited free** entertainment" | A "free / unlimited" claim must be fully true (CCPA Misleading Ads Guidelines 2022; ASCI). If there are ads, paid plans or limited titles, it is misleading. | **Removed.** Say "free" only if the app really costs nothing **and** every title on it is licensed. |
| 4 | "Na koi **fake pop-ups**, na koi **buffering**" | Absolute promises. Buffering depends on the user's internet; the reference app actually shows pop-up ads. | Changed to "smooth playback" (a normal feature line). |
| 5 | "**Install Now** button… **der mat kijiye**… abhi install karein" (3 times) | Pushy, and wrong if the app is not live yet (it is a sample / demo). | One calm call to action. Two endings are given: A for a live app, B for a pitch / demo. |
| 6 | "Hindi dubbing" of Hollywood / South films | The same rights problem as #1. Dubbing rights also belong to the film owner. | Changed to "apni bhasha chuno" (an app setting, no film promise). |

**OK to keep:** language choice, HD quality, subtitles, smooth playback, download + offline viewing, a simple home screen, one-tap play.
Use these only if the app really has them.

## 4. Before you post a promotion
- The app must show only content you have the rights to: your own films, licensed titles, or public-domain films.
- Use original posters and art (like the ones in `elements/`), not real film posters.
- If it is still a sample app, use ending **B** ("concept"), not "install now".
- If you later add "free", add the truth with it, for example "free, ads ke saath".

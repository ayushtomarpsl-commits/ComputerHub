## What changed from v1 (client draft)
1. **Result first.** The reel opens on the finished café website, not on a question. People see the payoff in second 1.
2. **Open loop.** "End में कीमत बताऊँगा" makes people wait until the price reveal near the end.
3. **"3 minutes" instead of "5 minutes".** This is now proven: in the recording it took ~2 min 20 s from prompt to finished site.
4. **The real demo story:** type the prompt → AI asks questions → plan → **Approve** → generating (with the game) → result.
5. **Comment bait.** The CTA asks two things: "WEBSITE" for the link, and "which business would you build a website for?" Both drive comments.
6. **Customisation is one short line.** The edits (dark green theme etc.) were not recorded, so we don't promise on screen what we can't show.

## Notes for any LLM / voice tool you give this to
- Keep the Hinglish style: Hindi in Devanagari, tech words in English (website, menu, AI Builder, Approve).
- Keep numbers as words where they are spoken ("उनहत्तर रुपये", "तीन मिनट", "तीस दिन"), so the voice reads them right.
- Do not add claims that are not in the list below. Do not say "free domain" or "free SSL" until MilesWeb confirms them.
- Keep the "end में कीमत" promise: the price must come only in beat 9.
- Target length 75–80 s. If it runs long, shorten beat 7 (the section list), not the hook or the CTA.

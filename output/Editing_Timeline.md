## Audio mix
- Voice: your original MP3, unchanged words. Only clean gain + a transparent peak limiter, to about −14 LUFS
  (true peak −1 dBTP). No EQ, no effects.
- Music: generated for this reel. Soft ambient synth pads (D major chords, 7.5 s each), gentle bell texture, airy
  noise, reverb. No drums, no beat. About 19 dB below the voice (around 10% perceived level), plus 5 dB automatic
  ducking whenever the voice is speaking. It rises a little in pauses and fades out at the end.
- Sound effects: 34 subtle synthesized events (whoosh, pop, click, chime, alert, rise, shimmer, swipe), only on
  scene changes and important moments. Not on every caption. About 15 LU below the voice.

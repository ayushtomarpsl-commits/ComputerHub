Laptop Doctor · Day 2 🩺 Used MacBook (M1 / M2 Air) ke 5 checks:
1️⃣ Erase Test: seller aapke saamne "Erase All Content and Settings" kare. Purane owner ki Apple ID maange = Activation Lock = deal khatam ❌
2️⃣ Company laptop? Setup mein "Remote Management" dikhe to mat lo, company use door se lock ya erase kar sakti hai 🔒
3️⃣ Battery: Battery Health "Service Recommended" ya cycle count 1,000 ke paas ho to battery ka kharcha price se kam karwao 🔋
4️⃣ Serial number: About This Mac = laptop ke neeche chhapa serial. Phir Apple ki Check Coverage site par model match karo 🔎
5️⃣ Hardware: saare ports, webcam, speakers, aur safed screen par daag ✅
⚠️ MacBook mein RAM aur storage baad mein nahi badhti. 8 GB liya to 8 GB hi rahega.

₹60,000 ka laptop 2 saal mein bekaar? Dukaan ki ye 5 lines sunte hi ruk jao 🛑

1️⃣ "i5 hai sir, sabse fast!" → number dekho. i5-12xx = 2022 ka processor, aaj ke daam par.
2️⃣ "8GB kaafi hai, baad mein badha lena" → kai laptops mein RAM soldered hoti hai, badhti hi nahi. 16GB se kam mat lo.
3️⃣ "Full HD display hai!" → Full HD sabmein hai. Nits poochho: 250 = pheeki screen, 300+ lo.
4️⃣ "Graphics card hai, gaming chalegi!" → Intel UHD / Iris Xe alag card nahi, processor ka hi hissa hai.
5️⃣ "MRP ₹75,000, aapke liye sirf ₹52,000!" → MRP sirf chhapa hua number hai. Model number ki photo lo, wahin online daam dekho.

⏱️ 10-second test (dukaan ke demo laptop par): Ctrl + Shift + Esc → Performance → CPU, Memory, GPU. Teen jhooth pakde gaye.

📩 Ye reel us dost ko bhejo jo laptop lene wala hai.

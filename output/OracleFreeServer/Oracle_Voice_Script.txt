Oracle tum sabko abhi free cloud server de raha hai, wo bhi 12 GB of RAM, 2 CPUs aur 200 GB of storage ke saath.
To haan, iska matlab ye hai ki ab tum 24 ghante apne saare apps aur automations run kar sakte ho, wo bhi tab, jab tumhara laptop band hai.
Par end mein wo ek galti bataunga, jisse ye free server 7 din mein wapas chhin sakta hai.
Aur haan, purani videos 24 GB bolti hain, par ab official limit 12 GB hai.

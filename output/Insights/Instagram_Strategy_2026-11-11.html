4Trial reel pehle, ya normal post?

Trial reel = pehle sirf non-followers ko dikhti hai, profile grid pe nahi aati. Accha chale to baad mein followers ko share kar sakte ho.

Normal post karo jab…

  • Strong series reel ho (Awesome Repos with paisa/free hook, Plan B kamaai idea)
  • Comment-keyword auto-DM laga ho (CreatorFlow "next post" trigger normal post pe pakka chalta hai)
  • Client / paid promo ho (MilesWeb) — client ko grid pe chahiye + Paid partnership label

Trial reel use karo jab…

  • Hook test: same reel, 2 alag hooks — dono trial, jo jeete use normal repost karo
  • Naya format / risky topic (pure dev tool, repeat topic jaise UPI v2)
  • Us din ki doosri reel (12:30 PM) — taaki main reel ka reach na kate

Mera answer

Hamare 77–99% views pehle se non-followers se aate hain, to main reels normal hi post karo. Trial reel ko testing tool ki tarah use karo — har hafte 1–2. Trial reel pe CreatorFlow auto-DM chalta hai ya nahi, ek baar test kar lena (specific post trigger se).

7Reach, engagement aur followers kaise badhayein

Reach

  • Pehla frame pe bada text hook + pehli line mein paisa/number ("₹23,600 har mahine")
  • Ek din ek reel, 6+ ghante gap
  • Repeat topic mat karo — naya angle ho tabhi
  • Intro "Welcome to…" 1.5 sec se kam

Engagement

  • ONE CTA per reel (viral rules) — keyword comment sabse strong
  • Pehle 60 min mein har comment ka reply (tum khud, 1 line)
  • Pinned comment: link page + keyword
  • Caption ki pehli line = hook, end mein ek sawaal

Followers

  • Series banao — "Day 1/2/3" logon ko follow karne ki wajah deta hai (Plan B ka follows/1K sabse zyada)
  • Outro mein follow ka reason: "roz ek free tool jo paisa bachaye"
  • Profile: 3 pinned reels = UPI (proof), GST (idea), ek client case study
  • Highlights: Free Tools · Plan B · Work/Clients · Hire me

Boost (paid) — sirf smart tarike se

  • Dashboard mein ₹1,200 ka boost draft pada hai — abhi finish mat karo
  • Boost sirf us reel ka karo jo leads laaye (case study), goal "Messages" — ₹300–500 test
  • Viral reel ko boost karna paisa waste hai, wo already free reach le chuki
